# Third-party models and binaries

## basic-pitch/

Basic Pitch note-transcription model (`model.json`, `group1-shard1of1.bin`), unmodified,
from `@spotify/basic-pitch` 1.0.1 — https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch-ts.
Copyright Spotify AB. Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (see `basic-pitch/LICENSE`).

Bittner, R. M., Bosch, J. J., Rubinstein, D., Meseguer-Brocal, G., & Ewert, S. (2022).
A Lightweight Instrument-Agnostic Model for Polyphonic Note Transcription and Multipitch
Estimation. ICASSP 2022.
