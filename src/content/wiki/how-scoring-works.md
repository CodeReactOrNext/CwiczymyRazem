Every session you log, every song you learn and every quest you finish pays you something. Understanding what pays what takes about two minutes, and it explains most of the decisions the app makes for you.

## Two currencies, two jobs

## Ways to earn points

<StatRow stats="+1:for every 3 minutes practised|+40:for learning a song|+10:for finishing your daily quests|+100:for clearing a Scale Map box" caption="Healthy habits add up to 5 more points per session, and the streak bonus multiplies the whole thing." />

- **Practice time** — every 3 minutes you log is worth 1 point. Technique, theory, hearing and creativity all pay the same.
- **Healthy habits** — tick things like warming up or using a metronome when you log a session; each one is worth a point, up to 5.
- **Learning a song** — marking a song as Learned pays +40, but only once you've logged at least 10 minutes of practice on that song. Mark it early and the status changes without the points. Move it back out of Learned and the +40 comes back off.
- **Daily quests** — finishing all three of a day's tasks pays +10 points and +40 Fame, once you claim it.
- **Scale Map** — clearing a full box on the scale tree pays a one-time 100 points and 50 Fame.

Rating a song's difficulty pays **+3 Fame** the first time you rate that song — no points. It's one of the small, steady Fame sources, alongside thanks from people who play the exercises you shared.

## The streak multiplier

Practise on consecutive days and every session that day is worth more. The bonus applies to your practice time *and* your habit points, and it's the closest thing the app has to a cheat code.

<ProgressLadder items="Day 1::The streak starts — no bonus yet|Day 2::+20% on everything you log that day|Day 3::+30%|Day 4::+40%|Day 5 and beyond::+50%, the maximum" highlight="Day 5 and beyond" caption="Miss a day and it goes back to zero. A 10-minute session is enough to keep it." />

<BlogAlert type="tip">
Showing up for ten minutes on a busy day beats a three-hour Sunday. The streak rewards frequency, not heroics.
</BlogAlert>
