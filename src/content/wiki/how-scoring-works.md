Every time you log a practice session, learn a song, or complete a daily quest, you earn points. Points drive your level, the current season's leaderboard, and your overall progress on riff.quest.

<BlogAlert type="info">
riff.quest actually runs on two separate currencies. **Points** decide your level and every leaderboard (all-time, seasonal, gear). **Fame** is spent in the [Arsenal](/wiki/arsenal-and-gear) on loot boxes, and is what season top-5 finishes, community exercise "thanks", and weekly Milestones pay out in. Some actions award one, some award both — this article covers points; Fame sources are called out explicitly where they overlap.
</BlogAlert>

## Ways to earn points

<StatRow stats="+1:point per 3 min practiced|+40:learn a song|+10:daily quests completed|+100:scale journey reward" caption="Points from healthy habits stack on top — up to +5 per session report." />

- **Practice time** — every 3 minutes of logged practice is worth 1 point, no matter the category (technique, theory, hearing, creativity all count the same).
- **Healthy habits** — check off things like warm-up, metronome use, or recording yourself when logging a session, up to 5 points per report.
- **Learn a song** — marking a song as Learned awards +40, but only once you've logged at least 10 minutes of practice time against that song — mark it Learned before that and the status changes with no points. Moving it back out of Learned takes the +40 back.
- **Daily quests** — complete all 3 tasks on your dashboard and claim the reward.
- **Scale Journey** — mastering a full scale position on the Scale Tree unlocks a one-time reward of **100 points and 50 Fame** — see [Journey & Scale Tree](/wiki/journey-and-scale-tree).

Rating a song's difficulty (first rating only) pays **+3 Fame**, not points — it's one of the smaller Fame sources alongside community exercise "thanks". See [Songs, Playlists & Difficulty](/wiki/songs-playlists-and-difficulty).

<BlogAlert type="tip">
Consistency beats marathon sessions — the streak multiplier below rewards showing up daily far more than one long weekend binge.
</BlogAlert>

## Streak multiplier

Practicing on consecutive days multiplies your whole session (time + habit points) — skip a day and the streak resets to zero.

<Checklist items="Day 1::No bonus yet — this starts the streak|Day 2::+20% on the day's points|Day 3::+30%|Day 4::+40%|Day 5+::+50%, the maximum bonus" />

## What points do

- **Levels** — your total points decide your level; each level needs progressively more.
- **Seasons** — every point also counts toward the current season's fresh monthly leaderboard.
- **Leaderboard** — points place you among all riff.quest guitarists, alongside everyone else's current season total.
