Points are your record. They set your level, they put you on the rankings, and you can never spend them on anything — which is the whole idea. They only ever go up, and they only ever measure work you actually did.

The app's other currency, **Fame**, is a completely separate pile that you *can* spend. It has its own article: [Fame & where it comes from](/wiki/fame).

## Ways to earn points

<StatRow stats="+1:for every 3 minutes practised|+40:for learning a song|+10:for finishing your daily quests|+100:for clearing a Scale Map box|+100:for a monthly challenge recording" caption="Healthy habits add up to 5 more points per session, and the streak bonus multiplies your practice time and habits on top of that." />

- **Practice time.** Every 3 minutes you log is worth 1 point. Technique, theory, hearing and creativity all pay the same.
- **Healthy habits.** Tick things like warming up or using a metronome when you log a session; each one is worth a point, up to 5.
- **Learning a song.** Marking a song as Mastered pays +40, but only once you've logged at least 10 minutes of practice on that song. Mark it early and the status changes without the points. Move it back out of Mastered and the +40 comes back off.
- **Daily quests.** Finishing all three of a day's tasks pays +10 points, once you claim it.
- **Scale Map.** Clearing a full box on the scale tree pays a one-time 100 points.
- **Monthly challenges.** Every accepted recording on the current month's board pays 100 points, so a full board of five is 500. Recordings filed against a closed month don't pay.

## The streak multiplier

Practise on consecutive days and every session that day is worth more. The bonus applies to your practice time *and* your habit points, and it's the closest thing the app has to a cheat code.

<ProgressLadder items="Day 1::The streak starts, with no bonus yet|Day 2::+20% on everything you log that day|Day 3::+30%|Day 4::+40%|Day 5 and beyond::+50%, the maximum" highlight="Day 5 and beyond" caption="Miss a day and it goes back to zero. A 10-minute session is enough to keep it." />

<BlogAlert type="tip">
Showing up for ten minutes on a busy day beats a three-hour Sunday. The streak rewards frequency, not heroics.
</BlogAlert>
