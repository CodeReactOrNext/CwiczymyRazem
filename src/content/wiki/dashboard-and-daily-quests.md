Home is the screen you land on. It's built to answer one question in about five seconds: **what should I do today?**

## Daily quests

Every day you get three small tasks. They're drawn at random from a big pool, and never overlap — you won't get "Rate a Song" and "Rate 3 Songs" on the same day. Finish all three and a Claim button appears.

<QuestPreview tasks="Practice for 15 minutes::done|Rate a Song::0 / 1|Practice 3 different categories::2 / 3" reward="+10 points, +40 Fame" caption="Tap any unfinished task and the app takes you straight to the screen where you can do it." />

The tasks are things you were probably going to do anyway: practise for a while, spend ten minutes on ear training, complete a routine, rate a song, add a song to your list. They tick off automatically as you practise and log — there's nothing to mark by hand.

<BlogAlert type="warning">
The reward isn't given automatically. You have to press Claim before the day rolls over — the quests reset with a fresh set of three the next day.
</BlogAlert>

## Following your progress

Home shows today. **Progress** shows everything else — your practice history day by day, how your time splits across the four categories, your longest streaks and your session log with every session you've ever saved.

<ClickPath steps="Progress" caption="Your history, statistics and full practice log — including the ability to edit or delete a session you logged wrong." />
