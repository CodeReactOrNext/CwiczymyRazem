**Fame** is the money. It buys cases, gear, parts and mods in the Arsenal, and it does nothing else — no level, no rank, no effect on your [points](/wiki/how-scoring-works). You can't buy it with real money, and nothing in the app is gated behind having a lot of it.

It comes from far more places than points do. Points have one real source, practice; Fame has about a dozen, and several of them pay you while you're asleep.

## Practice pays Fame as well

This surprises people who assume practice is points-only. It isn't: every session you log pays Fame too, just on a different shape of curve.

<StatRow stats="12:for 15 minutes in a day|16:for 30 minutes|23:for an hour|33:for two hours|46:for four hours" caption="Rounded, and these are Fame, not points. Notice how the gaps shrink: the first fifteen minutes pay better per minute than the fourth hour." />

Three things are worth understanding about that curve, because they change how you'd play it:

<Checklist items="It counts your day, not your session::Splitting two hours into six short reports pays exactly the same as logging it in one go. There is nothing to game here|Short and daily beats long and rare::The rate per minute falls the longer the day gets, which is the opposite of how points work. An hour a day for a week pays far more Fame than seven hours on Sunday|There's a daily ceiling::The practice curve stops paying somewhere past six hours in one day. The fixed rewards below are added on top of it and aren't affected" />

On top of the curve there are two modifiers:

<StepList steps="Showing up::A flat daily bonus once your streak is running — a few Fame from 3 days, more from 7, more again from 30. Paid once a day, on the first session that earns anything|Playing accurately::A session scored through the microphone at 85% or better has its practice Fame multiplied. It's the one place accuracy pays you anything at all|Logging late::A back-dated session pays a small fixed amount instead of going through the curve, since the day it belongs to has already been counted" />

## Fixed rewards

These are the big, predictable payouts. Most of the Fame that ends up buying a Legendary comes from here.

<StatRow stats="+5000:winning a season|+500:a monthly challenge recording|+750:bonus for clearing all five|+40:finishing your daily quests" caption="A full challenge board is 3250 Fame in a month and takes an afternoon; topping the season pays 5000 and takes the whole month." />

<Checklist items="Monthly challenges::500 Fame per accepted recording, plus 750 once every song on the board has one. Recordings on a closed month's board pay nothing|Weekly Milestones::Each level you've unlocked pays out every week you meet its goal, from 20 Fame a week at the bottom to 300 at the top, and every unlocked level you've met pays at once|Daily quests::40 Fame for finishing all three, once you press Claim|Scale Map::50 Fame each time you clear a full box, alongside the points|Season placings::Finishing a month in the top ten pays 5000 / 3000 / 2000 / 1200 / 700 Fame for the first five places and 500 / 400 / 300 / 200 / 150 for the rest, paid when the season closes. Winning a season is the single biggest payout in the app" />

## The community pays you

This is the part people miss. Once you've put something out there, other players' actions credit your account without you doing anything.

<StatRow stats="+20:someone saves your playlist|+5:someone likes your playlist|+5:a thank-you on an exercise you shared|+1:someone likes or comments on your recording" caption="Plus 1 Fame the first time another player completes an exercise you wrote. Your own plays and likes never count." />

<BlogAlert type="tip">
This is the closest thing to income the app has. Publish the exercises you write and the playlists you build once, and they keep paying long after you've forgotten about them. A playlist that catches on is worth more over a year than most of the fixed rewards.
</BlogAlert>

There's one thing you get paid for doing yourself: **rating a song's difficulty** pays 3 Fame the first time you rate a given song. Small, but it costs you five seconds and the ratings are what make the difficulty tiers useful for everyone else.

## And selling

Everything in the Arsenal can go back out again. Scrapping gear breaks it into parts, selling it pays Fame outright, and the Market lets you put a listing up at your own price for another player to buy. Gear you'll never equip is Fame you haven't collected yet.

## What Fame doesn't do

<Checklist items="It doesn't touch your level::Levels come from points, and no amount of Fame moves one|It isn't on the main rankings::The season and all-time boards count points only. The gear board is the only one Fame reaches, and only through what you've equipped|It isn't required::You can practise, log, learn songs, keep a streak and climb the rankings without ever opening the Arsenal|It can't be bought::There's no way to convert real money into Fame" />
