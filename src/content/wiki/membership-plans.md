riff.quest works without paying. The whole game layer — practising, logging, points, levels, streaks, songs, seasons, rankings, challenges and the entire Arsenal — is free, and stays free. The two memberships add practice **tools**, not shortcuts to the top of the leaderboard.

Both paid plans are cheaper billed yearly, and both start with a 7-day free trial. The trial is one per account — if you've used it once, upgrading later starts billing straight away.

## Which one is for you

<Checklist items="Free::You want to track what you practise, learn songs, chase a streak and collect gear. Most players never need more than this|Practice Pro::You want to build and follow your own practice routines, use the ready-made plans, and practise from Guitar Pro files|Practice Master::You want the app to plan for you — automatically generated sessions, AI-guided practice, personal skill roadmaps and the weekly analytics that come with them" />

## What Master unlocks in practice

<StepList steps="Auto Plan::Tell it how long you've got and it builds the session for you — see Exercise Plans & Auto Plan|Mastery Roadmaps::A personal, step-by-step curriculum generated for your level|Weekly Milestones::The weekly goal ladder that pays out Fame for keeping a consistent week|Deeper stats::Daily practice insights, a weekly summary and goal-based analytics" />

## Billing questions

<FaqList items="Where do I upgrade?::Any locked feature has an Upgrade button, or open the membership screen from your profile menu|What happens if I cancel?::You keep the paid features until the end of the period you already paid for, then the account goes back to Free. Nothing you earned is removed — points, gear, songs and history all stay|Do paid plans give more points?::No. Points come from practice only. A Master member and a free member who practise the same amount earn exactly the same|Can I switch from Pro to Master?::Yes — the upgrade screen switches the subscription over and prices it from your current plan" />
