Open **Practice** and you get a wall of options. They all end in the same place, a logged session that feeds your points, streak and skills, but they differ in how much thinking you have to do first.

## What each mode actually is

<Checklist items="Practice Routines::Ordered lists of exercises you follow one by one: the app's ready-made plans, playalongs, your own plans, and plans shared by other players|Free Timer::A stopwatch per skill category. Run it while you play, stop it when you're done|Manual Log::The same log form without the stopwatch, for sessions that happened away from the screen|Auto Plan::A session generated to fit the time you have|Guitar Pro Files::Practise from tabs you import, with playback|Songs::Track time against a specific song you're learning, and that time also counts towards learning it|Skills::23 tracked guitar skills, each with exercises that feed it|Exercises::The full exercise library to browse, plus everything shared by the community|Learning Path::Guided modules that unlock step by step|Mastery Roadmaps::A personal curriculum generated for your level|Scale Map::A fretboard tree of scale shapes, cleared box by box at a target tempo" />

## The four categories

Wherever you practise, your time is filed under Technique, Theory, Hearing or Creative work. They're worth exactly the same in points; the split exists so you can see what you're neglecting. Guided modes tag it for you, and the Free Timer and manual log leave it to you.

The full breakdown of what belongs where, and which skills each one feeds, is in **[The Four Categories](/wiki/the-four-categories)**.
