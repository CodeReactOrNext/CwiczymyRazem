Open **Practice** and you get a wall of options. They all end in the same place — a logged session that feeds your points, streak and skills — but they differ in how much thinking you have to do first.

<ClickPath steps="Practice" caption="Everything below lives on this one screen." />

## Pick by how much direction you want

## What each mode actually is

<Checklist items="Practice Routines::Ordered lists of exercises you follow one by one — the app's ready-made plans, playalongs, your own plans, and plans shared by other players|Free Timer::A stopwatch per skill category. Run it while you play, stop it when you're done|Manual Log::The same log form without the stopwatch, for sessions that happened away from the screen|Auto Plan::A session generated to fit the time you have. Master members only|Guitar Pro Files::Practise from tabs you import, with playback. Pro and Master members|Songs::Track time against a specific song you're learning — that time also counts towards learning it|Skills::22 tracked guitar skills, each with exercises that feed it|Exercises::The full exercise library to browse, plus everything shared by the community|Learning Path::Guided modules that unlock step by step|Mastery Roadmaps::A personal curriculum generated for your level. Master members only|Scale Map::A fretboard tree of scale shapes, cleared box by box at a target tempo" />

## The four categories

Wherever you practise, your time is filed under one of four categories. They're worth exactly the same in points — the split exists so you can see what you're neglecting.

<TierScale items="Technique::Hands, speed, accuracy, physical control|Theory::Scales, chords, harmony, reading|Hearing::Ear training, recognising intervals and chords|Creativity::Improvising, writing, arranging" caption="Your Progress screen shows the split, and several daily quests ask you to balance it." />

<BlogAlert type="tip">
Guided modes (routines, Auto Plan, roadmaps) tag the categories for you. If you're using the Free Timer or the manual log, tag honestly — this split is the only thing that tells you you've done no ear training in three weeks.
</BlogAlert>
