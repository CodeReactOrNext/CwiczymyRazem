Two structured, node-by-node learning paths live outside the free-form practice log: **Journey** and **Scale Tree**.

## Journey / Learning Path

`/journey` currently has two real modules — **Fundamentals** and **Fretboard Mastery** — plus a few more (rhythm, scales, improvisation) shown as locked/coming soon. Each module is a series of stages, and each stage is a series of steps with instructions, checklists, tips, and an exam at the end.

<StepList steps="Step 1 always open::Every module's first step is available immediately, no prerequisites|Sequential unlocking::Each later step only unlocks once the step directly before it (by order) is marked completed|Modules aren't gated::Both real modules are open from the start — locking only happens at the step level" />

**Fretboard Mastery** uses a "Click Hunt" mechanic instead of listening for notes through the mic — you're shown a note name and click where it lives on the fretboard. Exams (in any module) are graded by accuracy:

<StatRow stats="3 stars:95%+ accuracy|2 stars:90%+ accuracy|1 star:80%+ accuracy|Fail:below 80%, no stars awarded" />

## Scale Tree

`/scale-tree` is a node graph (nine clusters: minor and major pentatonic, natural minor, major, and the five modes — dorian, phrygian, mixolydian, lydian, locrian) covering every practical fretboard position for each scale.

Inside a cluster, each fretboard position has a chain of **7 pattern nodes** to clear — ascending, descending, ascending+descending, thirds, fourths, and two sequence patterns — each requiring you to hit a target tempo (80 BPM for pentatonic/major/minor scales, 75 BPM for the modes).

<BlogAlert type="tip">
Clearing all 7 pattern nodes for a position at or above the target BPM unlocks that position's **Reward node** — a one-time claim worth **100 points and 50 Fame**. You have to claim it explicitly; hitting the BPM target alone doesn't auto-grant it.
</BlogAlert>

Each node's status — locked, available, in progress, or completed — is driven by your recorded BPM progress on the exercise behind it, so the tree unlocks in order as you actually get faster, not just by time spent.
