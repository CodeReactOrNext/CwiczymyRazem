Rotation is your own list of things you don't want to lose: a song you finally nailed, an exercise that keeps your picking honest, a skill you worked hard for. You add them yourself, and the list keeps track of when you last played each one. Whatever has gone the longest without playing rises to the top, so the next thing to refresh is always waiting for you.

## Adding to your rotation

<ClickPath steps="Library|Rotation|Add" caption="Switch between Songs, Exercises and Skills, then tap anything to add it. Tap it again to take it out." />

Songs come from your own song library, with the ones you've mastered at the top. Exercises come from the whole exercise library. A skill doesn't play by itself: when its turn comes, you get one of the exercises that trains it, the one you've played least recently. Your rotation holds up to 60 things.

## Your own items

<ClickPath steps="Library|Rotation|Add|Own" caption="Give it a name, and add a note or a link if you like. Tap an example to start from it." />

Anything that isn't in the app can go in too: the next lesson of an online course, one new chord a day, a lick to transcribe by ear. The link can be a course page, a video or anything else on the web, and it sits right next to the item.

## Fresh, cooling down, getting rusty

Every item sits in one of three groups, and the five dots next to it drain day by day.

<StepList steps="Fresh::Played within the last week|Cooling down::Played one to three weeks ago|Getting rusty::Not played for three weeks or more, or not played at all since you added it" />

Anything you play counts, wherever you play it: a session from Rotation, a routine, an exercise from the library or a song you log by hand. An exercise counts once you've played it for 20 seconds, the same as everywhere else. Sessions you log for an earlier day don't move anything, because that day is already behind you.

## Playing from your rotation

<ClickPath steps="Library|Rotation|Play the 3 stalest" caption="Starts one short session with the three things you haven't played for the longest." />

The top of the page shows the next few items as a hand of cards and how many are waiting for a refresh. **Play the 3 stalest** puts them into one session. Songs get five minutes each there, and exercises keep their usual length. The play button on any row starts a session with just that one item.

**Draw a card** is for days when you want to play but don't want to pick. You get one random card, more often something you haven't played for a while, never the same card twice in a row. Play it or draw another.

A Rotation session is an ordinary practice session. It earns points, Fame and practice time the same way, and when it's done you land back on your rotation with everything re-sorted.
