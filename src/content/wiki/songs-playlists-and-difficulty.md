## What marking a song Learned pays

Moving a song to Learned pays **+40 points** — as long as you've logged at least **10 minutes** of practice on that specific song first. Mark it earlier and the status changes without paying anything. Move it back out of Learned and the +40 is taken back off. Either way, the change is mirrored into the current season's total.

<BlogAlert type="tip">
Practise the song from its own page — that's what builds up the 10 minutes, and it's also what tells the app which songs you actually work on.
</BlogAlert>

## Difficulty ratings and tiers

Anyone can rate a song from 1 to 10. Your first rating on a song pays **+3 Fame** (rating again is possible after a short cooldown, but only the first one pays). Everyone's ratings are averaged into the song's tier:

<TierScale items="D::Under 4 — beginner-friendly|C::4 to 6|B::6 to 7.5|A::7.5 to 9|S::9 and up — the hard stuff" caption="A song nobody has rated yet shows a '?' instead of a tier." />

## Skill Power

Skill Power is the app's one-number answer to "how hard is the stuff this person can actually play?" It's built from your **10 hardest Learned songs**, weighted so the hardest counts most, with a small bonus for having learned a lot of songs overall.

It's capped at the tier of your single hardest song, so a hundred easy songs can never out-rank one genuinely difficult one. You need at least **5 Learned songs** before it appears at all.

## Playlists

<Checklist items="Playlist::A free-form collection — a set list, a genre, songs for one technique|Path::An ordered journey through songs, meant to be worked through in sequence|Top::A ranked list, capped at 10 songs" />

Public playlists show up in Discover, ranked by how popular they are — someone saving a playlist counts three times as much as a like. Sharing pays: when someone saves your public playlist you get **+20 Fame**, and a like pays **+5 Fame**.
