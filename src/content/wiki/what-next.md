The app has a lot going on: daily quests, the exercise of the day, your streak, tempo goals, the calendar, Journey, roadmaps, songs, the monthly challenge, duels and more. What Next looks at all of it and lays it out as one road, so you never have to open five pages to decide what to play.

<ClickPath steps="What Next" caption="It sits right under Home in the menu. The same card is at the top of Home and on the screen after every session." />

## The next step

At the top is one thing to play now, with a line saying why it is there: two clean runs left to your next tempo milestone, a song you haven't touched for nine days, a streak that ends at midnight. **Play** takes you straight into it.

The next step is always something to play. Rewards, gear and anything you only open or read never take this spot. The one exception is a streak with only a few hours left: then keeping it alive comes first, because nothing else on the road is lost at midnight.

## Ready to collect

When rewards are waiting, they sit above the next step in one list: the daily quest, a weekly goal you met, badge rewards, finished scale tree boxes, guild quest rewards, unspent skill points and free cases. **Collect all** takes everything that can be collected right there in one tap. The rest links to the place where you collect it, for example the case shelf for a free case.

## The road below

Under the next step, everything else is sorted into four groups.

<StepList steps="Today::What runs out at the end of the day: your streak, daily quest tasks, the exercise of the day, duels waiting for your move and what your calendar has for today|In progress::Things you have started: tempo goals, the next Journey step, the step your roadmap is on, the skill tree, songs you are learning that have gone quiet, rusty items in your rotation and the plan you played last|This week and season::The weekly goal closest to done, the monthly challenge, the player just above you this season, a guild quest where your part is still open, and a part of practice you have skipped all week|On the horizon::Further goals: your next reward level and what it unlocks, badges you are close to and the next boss to take on" />

Inside each group, the order follows what matters most right now. A deadline that gets close moves up. A goal that is nearly finished moves up. Things you set up yourself, like a tempo goal, a calendar slot or a roadmap, come before suggestions. Three items in a row never come from the same place, so one long song list can't push everything else out of view.

## Play a session from the top

<ClickPath steps="What Next|15 min" caption="Under the next step, pick 15, 30 or 45 minutes." />

This packs the top of your road into one session of that length: the goal first, then the song, then the exercise of the day, and so on, until the time is used. It plays like any other session and earns points, Fame and practice time the usual way. When you finish, you log it and come back to an updated road.

## Not today

Every item has a menu with **Not today** and **Hide for a week**. A hidden item comes back on its own. If you open something and leave it unfinished, it steps back for a day so the next thing gets a turn.
