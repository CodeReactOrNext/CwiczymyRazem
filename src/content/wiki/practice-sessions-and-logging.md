A session that isn't logged never happened, as far as the app is concerned. Logging is the one habit everything else depends on: points, level, streak, skills and your whole practice history come out of this one form.

## Two ways to get there

## What the form asks for

It comes in two steps, and only the first one is compulsory: how long you played, split across the four categories.

<Screenshot src="/images/wiki/log-session-time.webp" alt="The 'Enter exercise time' step, with hour and minute wheels for Technique, Theory, Hearing and Creative work, a running total and a Save Now button" caption="The running total and Save Now sit at the bottom of this step, so a session can be logged and finished right here." />

The second step is optional in the fullest sense, since the button above lets you skip it entirely. It's where the session gets a name, and where you tick what you actually did.

<Screenshot src="/images/wiki/log-session-focus-habits.webp" alt="The 'Session focus & habits' step: a session title, tag groups for goal, playing style, techniques, theory, creative and performance, and a habits checklist with two boxes ticked" caption="Each ticked habit shows what it adds. The tag picker on the left just writes words into the title." />

The tags (goal, playing style, techniques, theory, creative, performance) do nothing clever, they simply add words to that title, so future-you can tell one Tuesday from another.

## Healthy habits

Each ticked habit is worth a point, up to five per session, and the streak bonus multiplies them along with everything else.

<Checklist items="Warm-up::You warmed up properly before playing|Metronome::You practised to a click|Learned something new::A new technique, section or concept|Exercise plan::You followed a structured routine instead of noodling|Recording::You recorded yourself playing" />

<BlogAlert type="tip">
Tick them honestly. They're worth a couple of points, but their real job is a nudge towards five habits that genuinely make practice better.
</BlogAlert>

## When the form pushes back

The form checks a few things before saving. It isn't trying to stop you, it's trying to keep your statistics worth looking at.

<Checklist items="No time at all::Won't save, since a session needs at least some minutes on it|24 hours or more::Won't save, no way around it|More than 6 hours::Saves, but asks you to confirm first|More than the day can hold::If the day's total would end up impossible, it asks you to confirm before saving" />

## Your practice history

Everything you've logged is kept, grouped by day, on your Progress screen. Sessions are labelled by where they came from: a manual entry, a routine, or a song you were practising.

<ClickPath steps="Progress|Practice Log" caption="Filter by date range, by session type or by length, and edit or delete anything you logged wrong." />

The summary at the top adds it up for you: total time, number of sessions, points earned, active days, average session length and how your time split across the four categories.
