## Skills

`/profile/skills` tracks **22 individual skills** spread across the same 4 categories used everywhere else in riff.quest — technique, theory, hearing, creativity. Every exercise you complete as part of a logged session feeds XP into whichever skills it's tagged with; there's no cap or level-up threshold, it's a straightforward running total per skill that only ever goes up.

<BlogAlert type="info">
The same underlying number shows up labeled two different ways depending on where you look — as raw "XP" on an individual skill card, and as a "Level" on the category summary card. It's the same value either way.
</BlogAlert>

## Achievements

Achievements are unlockable badges, not a points/Fame source — they don't pay out any currency, they're purely collectible recognition of things you've done. Each achievement defines its own unlock condition (things like hitting a streak, logging a certain amount of time, or completing specific milestones), checked automatically against your session and profile data whenever relevant. Achievements have a rarity — common, rare, very rare, or epic — reflected in their badge color.
