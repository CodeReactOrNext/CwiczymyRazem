Guitarists who learned these songs rate Seven Nation Army as the easiest famous guitar song at 2.4 out of 10, followed by Highway to Hell (3.0), House of the Rising Sun (3.3), Come As You Are (3.7) and Smoke on the Water (3.8). The hardest of the 29 is Tornado of Souls by Megadeth at 9.8, where every one of its 12 raters gave a 9 or a 10. Master of Puppets scores 7.6, Stairway to Heaven 6.6 and Hotel California 4.7, lower than its reputation suggests. The scores are averages of 370 ratings left in the Riff Quest song library by players after they learned each song, counted on 27 November 2026. Each row of the chart also shows how many players rated the song and the lowest and highest vote, because an average of 3 ratings and an average of 25 are not the same kind of number.

## Where the Scores Come From

Every score in this chart comes from the [Riff Quest song library](/song-library). When a player marks a song as learned, the app asks how hard it was on a scale from 1 to 10, and the average of those answers is the song's difficulty score.

<StatRow stats="29:songs in this chart|370:ratings from players|13:ratings per song on average" caption="Riff Quest song library, 27 November 2026" />

The 29 songs are the ones that have a full [song guide](/song-library) on Riff Quest. The selection is rock and metal that players put on their lists most often, so the chart says nothing about jazz, classical or fingerstyle repertoire.

Four songs in the chart have fewer than five ratings: The Trooper and Snow (Hey Oh) have 4 each, Eruption and Crazy Train have 3 each. Their scores are shown, but one more rating could move any of them by half a point or more.

The scores are grouped into five tiers. D means beginner (under 4), C intermediate (4 to 5.9), B advanced (6 to 7.4), A expert (7.5 to 8.9) and S legendary (9 and up). Seven of the 29 songs sit in D, twelve in C, seven in B, two in A and one in S.

## Six Songs Players Rated Easier Than I Did

Each song guide on Riff Quest carries a difficulty grade I set when I wrote the guide. On six of the 29 songs, players came in at least 0.9 points under my grade.

| Song | My grade | Players | Ratings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eruption | 9.5 | 7.3 | 3 |
| Seven Nation Army | 4.5 | 2.4 | 23 |
| Hotel California | 6.5 | 4.7 | 14 |
| Snow (Hey Oh) | 8.5 | 7.3 | 4 |
| Sweet Child O' Mine | 7.5 | 6.6 | 15 |
| Master of Puppets | 8.5 | 7.6 | 20 |

Seven Nation Army is the clearest miss. I graded Seven Nation Army 4.5 because the full song ends with a solo that is a separate, more advanced project, and 23 players put it at 2.4, with no vote above 5. The main riff takes a day, and that riff is what most players mean when they say they learned the song.

Hotel California shows the same pattern at a higher level. I graded Hotel California 6.5 with the harmonised outro solo in mind, and 14 players put it at 4.7. My reading of that gap is that most of them rated the chord part, which needs two clean barre chords and little else. The [Hotel California guide](/song-library/hotel-california) grades the rhythm part, the intro and the solo separately for that reason.

Eruption and Snow (Hey Oh) have the largest gaps and the least data. Eruption has three ratings that run from 3 to 10, and Snow (Hey Oh) has four that run from 3 to 9. A range that wide on so few votes means the average is not settled yet.

## Four Songs Players Rated Harder Than I Did

Players rated four songs at least 0.7 points harder than my grade: Thunderstruck (7.0 against my 6.0), Smoke on the Water (3.8 against 3.0), Enter Sandman (5.8 against 5.0) and Nothing Else Matters (5.3 against 4.6).

Enter Sandman has the highest floor in the middle of the chart. No player out of 12 rated Enter Sandman below 5. The palm-muted riff at about 123 BPM and the wah solo are what the full song asks for.

Nothing Else Matters scores 5.3 from 21 players, above the 4.6 I gave it. The open-string intro of Nothing Else Matters is slow and beginner-friendly, and the song grows into an intermediate fingerstyle piece after it. The [Nothing Else Matters guide](/song-library/nothing-else-matters) marks where the fingerpicking gets hard.

## Where Players Disagree With Each Other

A wide range of ratings usually means a song has an easy part everyone knows and a hard part fewer people finish. Smoke on the Water is the extreme case in this chart: 25 ratings that run from 1 to 10. The riff takes an afternoon, and the full song includes Ritchie Blackmore's solo, which is fast G minor pentatonic phrasing with bends.

House of the Rising Sun has the same shape, with 21 ratings from 1 to 9. The six chords are beginner shapes, and the obstacle is keeping the rolling 6/8 arpeggio going while the F chord arrives on time.

Master of Puppets runs from 3 to 10 across 20 ratings. The low votes are hard to explain for a song at 212 BPM with long stretches of downpicking, and the average of 7.6 is where most of the 20 ratings cluster.

Some songs get nearly the same rating from everyone. Tornado of Souls has 12 ratings and all of them are a 9 or a 10. Fade to Black runs only from 3 to 5 across 9 ratings, Californication from 4 to 6 across 14, and Seek & Destroy from 5 to 7 across 10. A narrow range means the song is about equally hard from start to finish, so the score can be trusted more.

## Tempo Does Not Predict the Score

A fast tempo does not make a song hard in this chart, and a slow one does not make it easy. Paranoid runs at about 163 BPM and scores 4.4, because the whole song is power chords. Everlong is at 158 BPM and scores 5.1.

The slowest songs in the chart are not at the bottom of it. Stairway to Heaven is at about 72 BPM and scores 6.6, and Hotel California is at 74 BPM and scores 4.7. Wish You Were Here, at about 60 BPM, scores 3.9.

Tempo matters when it comes with stamina. Master of Puppets at 212 BPM scores 7.6, The Trooper at 160 BPM scores 7.0, and Tornado of Souls at 196 BPM scores 9.8. All three ask the picking hand to hold that speed for minutes, not for one riff.

## How to Use the Chart to Pick a Song

The chart works best as a ladder. Find the hardest song you can already play cleanly from start to finish, then look about one point above it. A player who is comfortable with Come As You Are at 3.7 has a realistic next step in Creep (4.3), Paranoid (4.4) or Hotel California's rhythm part (4.7).

Jumps of three points or more are where songs get abandoned. Going from Smoke on the Water at 3.8 straight to Master of Puppets at 7.6 skips everything the songs between them teach: barre chords, palm muting at speed and alternate picking across strings.

Check the tuning before choosing. Come As You Are is in D standard, Smells Like Teen Spirit and Sweet Child O' Mine are a half step down, and Everlong is in drop D. Playing along with the record in standard tuning sounds wrong even when every fret is right.

## What These Numbers Cannot Tell You

The ratings come from players who chose to learn a song and finished it. A beginner who gave up on Master of Puppets after a week never rated it, so the hardest songs are probably rated by stronger players than the easiest ones, and their scores are likely lower than a random guitarist would give.

Players rate the version they learned. A score does not say whether the player learned the solo, played at full tempo or used a simplified arrangement, which is why the lowest and highest rating are printed next to every average.

The scores are a snapshot from 27 November 2026. The [song library](/song-library) always shows the current number, and the four songs with fewer than five ratings will move the most.

## FAQs

### What is the easiest famous song to play on guitar?

Seven Nation Army by The White Stripes has the lowest score of the 29 songs in this chart: 2.4 out of 10 from 23 players, with no rating above 5. Highway to Hell (3.0) and House of the Rising Sun (3.3) follow.

### Is Master of Puppets hard to play on guitar?

Yes. Master of Puppets scores 7.6 out of 10 from 20 players who learned it, which puts it in the expert tier. The song runs at 212 BPM and the difficulty is holding fast downpicking for more than eight minutes, not any single riff.

### Is Hotel California hard to play on guitar?

The chord part is intermediate and the solo is advanced. Hotel California scores 4.7 out of 10 from 14 players, lower than its reputation, most likely because many of them rated the rhythm part. The harmonised outro solo is a separate and much harder job.

### Is Stairway to Heaven hard to play on guitar?

Stairway to Heaven scores 6.6 out of 10 from 12 players, in the advanced tier, with ratings from 4 to 9. The fingerpicked intro is the accessible part, and the solo is where the high ratings come from.

### What is the hardest song in the chart?

Tornado of Souls by Megadeth, at 9.8 out of 10. All 12 players who rated Tornado of Souls gave it a 9 or a 10, the narrowest range of any song in the chart with more than three ratings.
